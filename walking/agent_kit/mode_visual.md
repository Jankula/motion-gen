- You can look at your result. `$python preview.py` renders `motion.npz` from the four cameras into `preview/contact_sheet.png`: one row per camera (front, back, left, right), one column per frame across a short time window. The default window is 1.2 s starting at 2 s; see `$python preview.py --help` to choose another one. Open the image to look at it. You can run `preview.py` at most $max_previews times in total, so use it deliberately. You may also check your motion numerically with your own code, for example using the forward kinematics in `SMPLX_INTERFACE.md`.
